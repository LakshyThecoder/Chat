# Product Requirements Document

## User problem
Consumers regularly encounter situations where they may be owed money, service, compensation, warranty coverage or a correction—but the process to recover it is scattered across documents, policies, emails, support flows and web forms.

## Product promise
Aegis reduces the work between “something went wrong” and “the problem is resolved.”

## Core jobs-to-be-done
1. “Tell me whether this looks wrong.”
2. “Show me what proves it.”
3. “Tell me what I can realistically do.”
4. “Prepare the action.”
5. “Ask me before doing something consequential.”
6. “Actually do it.”
7. “Keep watching until it is resolved.”

## Primary personas
### Busy consumer
Wants to recover value without spending an hour on support.

### Power consumer
Wants visibility, evidence and configurable autonomy.

### Future provider
Wants agent-ready capabilities that reduce support friction.

## Non-goals
- legal advice
- medical advice
- unbounded financial advice
- autonomous money transfers
- generic assistant replacement
