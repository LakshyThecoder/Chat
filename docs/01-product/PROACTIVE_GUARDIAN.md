# Proactive Guardian

## Purpose
Find meaningful consumer actions without creating notification fatigue.

## Signals
- duplicate charge
- charge after cancellation
- refund overdue
- warranty expiry approaching
- potential compensation
- provider requesting missing evidence
- inactive case needing follow-up

## Safety
A finding is a recommendation until evidence and policy checks support it.
High-confidence findings may trigger a prepared case, not an automatic high-impact action.

## UX
Use:
“I found something worth acting on.”
not:
“AI detected 17 anomalies.”
