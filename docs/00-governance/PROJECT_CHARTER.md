# Project Charter

## Mission
Create a credible agent-native consumer advocacy platform that demonstrates what becomes possible when humans and agents can collaborate through an open, capability-based web.

## Success
A stranger can submit a consumer problem and complete a verified resolution workflow without developer intervention.

## Winning requirements
- WebMCP is essential and visible.
- AI is necessary for ambiguity/reasoning.
- The product is useful without being a demo prop.
- The experience is premium and trustworthy.
- Execution is real within the controlled provider environment.
